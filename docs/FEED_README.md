### Scoring Algorithm Referansı

```typescript
Total Score (0-110) = Trust (0-40) + Inventory (0-30) + 
                      Engagement (0-20) + Recency (0-10) + Boost (0-10)

Trust:          40 (User trusts author)
                35 (Mutual trust)
                30 (Author trusts user)

Inventory:      30 (Exact product match)
                20 (Product group match)
                15 (Category match)

Engagement:     20 (Trending)
                15 (High engagement >0.7)
                0-10 (Normalized)

Recency:        10 (<48 hours)
                5 (<14 days)
                0 (>14 days)

Boost:          5-10 (Active boost)

Penalties:
- Seen:         score × 0.5
- Hide:         score × 0.3
- Save:         score + 10
```
